PENDUBOT MODEL AND CONTROLLERS
============================

1. Coordinates and force

x = [q1,q2,v1,v2]^T, v1=dq1/dt, v2=dq2/dt.
q1=0 points downward. q2 is the relative elbow angle, so the absolute
orientation of link 2 is q1+q2. The elbow is passive.

COM positions (horizontal, vertical upward):
p_c1 = [lc1 sin(q1), -lc1 cos(q1)]
p_c2 = [l1 sin(q1)+lc2 sin(q1+q2),
        -l1 cos(q1)-lc2 cos(q1+q2)]

Kinetic energy:
T = 1/2 m1 |dp_c1/dt|^2 + 1/2 I1 v1^2
  + 1/2 m2 |dp_c2/dt|^2 + 1/2 I2 (v1+v2)^2
  = 1/2 v^T M(q) v.

Potential energy:
V = -(m1 lc1 + m2 l1) g cos(q1) - m2 lc2 g cos(q1+q2).

The motor acts between ground and link 1, so its virtual work is
delta W = u delta q1. There is no torque at the elbow and no work term
proportional to delta q2. Thus the generalized force is [u,0]^T even
though q2 is relative. The passive elbow still moves through coupling.

2. Equations of motion

Apply d/dt(dL/dv_i) - dL/dq_i = tau_i with L=T-V.
Let b=m2 l1 lc2, d=I2+m2 lc2^2,
a=I1+m1 lc1^2+m2 l1^2+d.

M = [[a+2b cos(q2), d+b cos(q2)],
     [d+b cos(q2), d]]
c = [-b sin(q2)(2 v1 v2+v2^2), b sin(q2) v1^2]^T
G = [(m1 lc1+m2 l1)g sin(q1)+m2 lc2 g sin(q1+q2),
      m2 lc2 g sin(q1+q2)]^T

M(q) ddq + c(q,v) + G(q) = [u,0]^T
f(x,u) = [v; M(q)^(-1)([u,0]^T-c-G)].

For the specified parameters:
M11=5/3+cos(q2), M12=M21=1/3+0.5 cos(q2), M22=1/3.
c1=-0.5 sin(q2)(2v1v2+v2^2), c2=0.5 sin(q2)v1^2.
G1=14.715 sin(q1)+4.905 sin(q1+q2), G2=4.905 sin(q1+q2).

At rest with zero control all four combinations q1 in {0,pi},
q2 in {0,pi} are equilibria (and their periodic equivalents).
The target [pi,0,0,0] is the fully extended upright equilibrium.
In unforced motion, E=T+V should remain constant. With control, dE/dt=u*v1.

3. Discrete RK4 map

Input u is constant over dt. k1=f(x,u), k2=f(x+dt*k1/2,u),
k3=f(x+dt*k2/2,u), k4=f(x+dt*k3,u).
F(x,u)=x+dt*(k1+2k2+2k3+k4)/6.
JAX differentiates this complete map, including all intermediate RK stages.
Differentiating only the continuous f and approximating F_x by I+dt*f_x
would not be the derivative of the optimizer's RK4 map.

4. Cost and full DDP

g(x,u)=dt/2*((x-xgoal)^T Q (x-xgoal)+r*u^2),
h(xN)=1/2*(xN-xgoal)^T QN (xN-xgoal).

Initialize value derivatives at the final state:
V_x=QN*(xN-xgoal), V_xx=QN.

At each backward stage, A=F_x, B=F_u, and V derivatives refer to
the next-stage value evaluated at the nominal next state.

H_x = g_x + A^T V_x
H_u = g_u + B^T V_x
H_xx = g_xx + A^T V_xx A + sum_i V_x[i] * F_i,xx
H_ux = g_ux + B^T V_xx A + sum_i V_x[i] * F_i,ux
H_uu = g_uu + B^T V_xx B + sum_i V_x[i] * F_i,uu

Here g_x=dt Q (x-xgoal), g_u=dt r u, g_xx=dt Q,
g_ux=0, g_uu=dt r. These equations are obtained by the second-order
Taylor expansion of H = g + V_next(F(x+dx,u+du)). The terms involving
V_x and the Hessians of F are retained in full DDP. iLQR drops exactly
these terms. Both retain A^T V_xx A and the other first-derivative products.

The one-dimensional control Hessian is regularized:
H_uu_reg = H_uu + mu, required to be strictly positive.
k = inv(H_uu_reg) H_u; K = inv(H_uu_reg) H_ux.
The signs follow the project convention:
u_new = u_nom - alpha*k - K*(x_new-x_nom).

Substitution of du=-k-K dx into the unregularized quadratic model gives:
V_x = H_x - K^T H_u - H_ux^T k + K^T H_uu k
V_xx = H_xx - K^T H_ux - H_ux^T K + K^T H_uu K.
Symmetrize V_xx to limit roundoff.

Increase mu when the backward pass has an invalid control Hessian or
the forward line search fails; reduce mu after an accepted step.
Try alpha = 1,1/2,...,1/128 and accept only a finite, strictly lower cost.
Stop when relative cost reduction <1e-8 AND max |k| <1e-3 N m,
or identify a small stationary correction when no improving step exists
at low regularization. Other termination reasons remain visible in output.
All final feedback gains are recomputed about the final nominal solution.

5. Execution and upright stabilization

During the 6 s plan, use either u_nom or u_nom-K*(x-x_nom), sampled
at 0.02 s and held constant during each interval. Validate on the exact
same RK4 model, then on ten RK4 substeps per sample. This is a numerical
continuous-dynamics approximation, not an analytic continuous-time solution.

After 6 s, use u=-K_LQR*(x-xgoal), with K_LQR from the discrete Riccati
equation for A=F_x(xgoal,0), B=F_u(xgoal,0), Q=diag(100,100,10,10),
R=0.1 (both Q and R scaled by dt). Hold for 5 more seconds.
LQR is local and may fail when the swing-up misses its capture region.

All optimization errors are unwrapped, preserving the prescribed cost.
For physical success metrics only, angle errors are wrapped to [-pi,pi).
Swing-up: each angle error <0.05 rad and each velocity <0.10 rad/s at 6 s.
Hold: each angle error <0.10 rad and each velocity <0.25 rad/s throughout
the additional 5 s, including the switching instant.

There is no hard motor torque limit in the supplied problem or this code.
Peak torque is an outcome to report, not an actuator specification.
Do not add clipping and assume the same optimized trajectory is feasible.
